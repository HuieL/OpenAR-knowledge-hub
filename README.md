# OpenAR Knowledge Hub

A static, public registry of **conditional, traceable, reviewable research
knowledge** for OpenAR (`openar-cli` / `ora`).

A record here is one atomic finding, heuristic or recipe together with the
conditions it applies to, the scope it was actually tested in, the evidence it
rests on (pinned to exact versions and hashes), the assessments other people
made of it, and credit for everyone involved. It is **not** a log dump, a
benchmark leaderboard, or a source of settled truth.

OpenAR reads `knowledge.json` at a **pinned commit SHA**, verifies each
record's sha256, and keeps a read-only local snapshot. What a host does with a
record — ignore it, keep it searchable, adopt it for a task, or re-verify it —
is that host's own decision under its own policy. Nothing in this repository is
executed by a reader.

## Three separate questions

| Axis | Where it lives | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Publication | `publication.status` | `candidate` → `accepted` (catalogued) → `retracted` / `superseded`. Being catalogued says nothing about correctness. |
| Assessment | `assessments[]` | Who checked which exact statement and evidence, how (static check, content review, analysis recompute, independent rerun, transfer validation), with what result and scope. |
| Local adoption | the reader's host | Not stored here. A remote record never becomes "validated" on your machine by declaring it. |

There is deliberately no `validated: true` field. `authorReport` carries the
author's own local state as a self-report only.

## Layout

- `entries/knowledge/<id>/record.json` — the record (see `schemas/knowledge-record.schema.json`)
- `entries/knowledge/<id>/evidence/` — optional small hosted evidence (data, text,
  analysis scripts), every file listed with its sha256 in `record.files`
- `packages/<id>/package.json` — a **Knowledge Package**: several related records
  (by `id@revision`) published together with the evidence and experiments they
  share (`packages/<id>/evidence/`), dependencies, reproduction steps and what was
  withheld (see `schemas/knowledge-package.schema.json`). A record can point at
  shared evidence with a `{"type": "package", "package": "<id>", "path": "evidence/…"}`
  locator. Records stay the unit an agent retrieves; listing in this registry is
  not verification on anyone's host.
- `knowledge.json` — index generated by `scripts/build-index.py` (never hand-edited);
  packages are listed under a separate `packages` key
- `scripts/validate.py` — the record contract, hash checks, disclosure and
  sensitive-content scan, and (with `--base`) append-only history rules
- `.github/workflows/` — read-only validation CI (no secrets, nothing from a
  record is executed)

Large data, models and logs stay in versioned external storage (a GitHub
commit, a specific Zenodo *version* record) and are referenced by locator and
hash.

## Evidence disclosure levels

Each evidence item states what a reader can actually do with it:

- `summary` — a description only; not independently checkable
- `checkable-data` — the underlying data/outputs are readable at a pinned locator
- `reproducible-package` — code and data sufficient to rerun are readable at a pinned locator

A record whose evidence is all `summary` can still be shared as a marked lead,
but it must say how that limits verification.

## License

Repository scripts and schema: MIT (see `LICENSE`). Each record states its own
license in `record.license` (CC-BY-4.0 recommended); hosted evidence files are
covered by that record license unless the evidence item says otherwise.

Published content may persist in clones and caches; retraction is recorded,
but cannot recall copies others already hold.
